The lectureships

Two awards presented at each triennial meeting.

The ISH lectureship awards recognize scientific leadership, innovation and sustained service to the hantavirus field.

01

Joel M. Dalrymple Memorial Award

Recognizes an outstanding early- to mid-career investigator for innovative contributions to hantavirus research.

02

Lee Ho Wang Lifetime Achievement Award

Honors a senior investigator for exceptional and sustained contributions to the field.
