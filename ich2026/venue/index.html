Optional Santiago stopover

Only for participants planning time in Santiago.

A stay in Santiago is optional and is not required to reach ICH2026. Travellers continuing directly should connect at Santiago Airport (SCL) to a domestic flight to Puerto Montt (PMC).

The resources below may be useful if you choose to stay overnight or add personal travel in Santiago. These services are independent of ICH2026.
