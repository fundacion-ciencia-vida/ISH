ICH2026 updateAugust 4, 2026

ICH2026 deadlines, fellowships and award nominations

Several important ICH2026 opportunities and deadlines have been updated.

  • Final abstract submission: August 16, 2026. Oral presentations will be selected from submitted abstracts.
  • Fellowship applications: August 23, 2026, for researchers based in South America.
  • Early-bird registration: September 1, 2026.
  • Lectureship award nominations: September 4, 2026.

Registration is also open for the November 5 ANDV Workshop. Participants may attend the workshop without registering for ICH2026.

Hantavirus CORCVirtual open consultation

Vaccine Working Group open consultation

Following the recent Pre-Clinical Models Working Group discussion, the Hantavirus CORC is convening a Vaccine Working Group meeting for experts involved in hantavirus vaccine research and development.

DateThursday, September 10, 2026
Time13:00-15:00 CET2 hours
FormatOnline webinarZoom

Meeting objectives

  1. 01

    Share updates on current vaccine development activities.

  2. 02

    Identify key research priorities and remaining knowledge gaps.

  3. 03

    Explore opportunities for collaboration to strengthen global preparedness and response for hantavirus outbreaks.

Questions submitted through the Zoom chat will be reviewed and addressed during the discussion.

A detailed agenda will be circulated closer to the meeting date.

Convened by the R&D Blueprint on behalf of the Hantavirus CORC.
